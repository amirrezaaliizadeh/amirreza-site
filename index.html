About

I'm a second-year MSc student in Marketing and Global Management at the University of Milano-Bicocca. Before Milan I spent about six years working in Iran: negotiating sports sponsorships, leading sales at a smart-building automation company, managing a business school and working in media and communications.

Today I work across strategy, marketing and consumer behavior, with a particular interest in the gap between what consumers say they value and what they buy. I also publish content in Persian and in English.
